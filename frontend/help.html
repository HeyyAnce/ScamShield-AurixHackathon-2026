Does “safe” mean I can trust the sender?

No. It means the text did not show strong scam signals. Verify unexpected requests through an official channel before taking action.

Which languages can I check?

Submit English, Hindi or Hinglish messages. Use the language switch for English or Hindi interface and AI explanations. Offline explanations may remain in English.

Can I upload a screenshot?

Yes. Choose a clear PNG, JPEG or WebP image up to 5 MB. Extraction is experimental: review and correct the text before checking it, especially Hindi text.

What if the AI is unavailable?

The checker can use warning-sign rules and available reference examples. The result tells you which checks were used. A fallback result has limitations too.

Why did the result get something wrong?

Language and context are complicated. Both AI and rules can make mistakes. Treat every result as a second opinion, and verify important decisions independently.

Can you recover money or file a report for me?

No. Scam Shield does not recover money or submit official complaints. See the Safety Guide for the next steps and official reporting channels.
