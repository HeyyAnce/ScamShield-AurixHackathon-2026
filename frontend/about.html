About Scam Shield

Technology that helps
you think twice.

Built by Team IDEAFORGE for people navigating everyday messages, urgent demands and uncertain links.

A calmer moment
in a noisy inbox.

Scams often ask us to act before we think. Scam Shield creates space to pause: paste a message, see the warning signs and consider a safer next step.

Our prototype combines AI analysis, warning-sign rules and reference examples. The reference collection includes synthetic messages; it is not a verified record of real incidents or proof of accuracy.

We are a student hackathon team. This is an independent project, not a bank, police service or government-endorsed tool.

Privacy · prototype

Know what happens to your input.

Messages and screenshots

Checking sends your text to our Python backend. On the public site this runs on Streamlit Community Cloud. When AI is available, the message is sent to Google Gemini for analysis. Screenshot extraction sends the processed image to Gemini. Remove personal information before submitting. Third-party processing follows the provider’s policies.

Browser preferences and feedback

Your theme and language preferences are saved in this browser. Message text is not saved to browser storage. Feedback is downloaded only when you choose to save it; it is not submitted to a team inbox.

Prototype limits

This prototype keeps the latest request and result in your active server session. It does not intentionally save submissions to a database. Hosting and AI providers have their own retention policies. Do not submit confidential information. Feedback delivery is not connected yet.
